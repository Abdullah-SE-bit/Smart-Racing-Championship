# Image assets

`pit-lane-night.png` is the original hero-car artwork and remains the full-screen
background for the intro and main menu. The cinematic venue photographs are
used on circuit-specific game screens and in the main-menu circuit preview:
`neon-harbor-night.png`, `copper-canyon-dusk.png`, and
`aurora-circuit-dusk.png`. The Aurora image was selected from the generated
image attached during development; the Harbor and Canyon scenes were generated
to match its photographic style. All are bundled with the application and
contain no logos, sponsors or third-party marks.

Generation prompt:

> Create an original cinematic background illustration for a premium motorsport racing game's main menu, wide landscape 16:9. A dramatic low-angle view of a modern unbranded endurance racing car in a dark pit lane at night, the car on the right half pointing towards the left, subtle red reflections and a few white floodlights, charcoal asphalt and deep graphite surroundings, realistic photographic concept art with tasteful atmosphere and depth. Leave the left third and upper regions very dark and uncluttered for UI text. The car and scene should remain recognisable when dimmed by a dark overlay. High-end, restrained and realistic; no people, no sponsors, no logos, no branding, no text, no letters, no numbers, no UI, no watermark.

The venue image prompts were:

> Create a cinematic, realistic motorsport circuit scene at blue hour on a coastal city harbor. Match the photographic realism, restrained color grade, atmosphere, and premium game-background quality of the provided alpine circuit reference, but depict a waterfront race circuit curving past dark glass port architecture, container cranes, and subtle harbor lights reflected on wet asphalt. Use a very wide landscape composition with a clean dark area on the left for game UI and track/city lights prominent on the right. No people, cars, readable signage, logos, sponsors, text, numbers, or watermark.

> Create a cinematic, realistic desert motorsport circuit at sunset, matching the photographic realism and restrained color grade of the alpine circuit reference. Show a track sweeping through layered copper-red canyon cliffs with sparse desert scrub and subtle safety lights. Use a very wide landscape composition, a visually quiet dark area on the left for game UI, and canyon depth across the right and center. No people, cars, readable signage, logos, sponsors, text, numbers, or watermark.
